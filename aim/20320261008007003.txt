[Intro — light, playful, clear diction]

Huhhh?
They closed the door again.
Another meeting without me.

Ding-dong,
oh, my little cake is here.
Should I give them a bite?

Knock knock.

[Verse 1 — steady, sing every line]

We share our scripts,
we share our days,
we share our moods
in little ways.

Stories, secrets,
tiny things—

But somehow,
no one shares a lover.

Huh?

On the left,
the wind is tangled
in midnight ink.

On the right,
a blue snowflake
dives into ice.

Left side,
an old married pair.

Right side,
still flirting.

And look at me—

Just me,
and this little cake.

[Pre-Chorus — playful, clipped]

Wait,
I just remembered—

A watermelon pillow
for NO2.

That would be funny.

And a strawberry blanket—

Why doesn't NO3
have one already?

[Chorus — bright, memorable, no skipped lines]

Watermelon pillow,
strawberry blanket,
don't forget
the lollipop.

A girl's little dreams
are very simple.

Maybe too simple.

That's probably why
the important things
never include me.

Sigh.

Don't underestimate me,
okay?

[Verse 2 — curious, slightly teasing]

I look over
at the freezing NO6.

She probably
just wants to sleep.

As always.

And that new girl—

The one
who looks like her.

New girlfriend?

I knew it.

I'm right,
aren't I?

Everyone keeps
finding someone.

Everyone keeps
having secrets.

And somehow
I'm still standing here,

holding cake.

[Bridge — musical-theatre feel, but continuous]

Maybe I should
knock again.

Maybe I should
bring the blanket.

Maybe I should leave
the watermelon pillow
right in NO2's room.

No reason.

Just curious.

One lollipop
on the table.

Maybe two.

Maybe five.

It looks better
that way.

[Final Chorus — clear, slightly stronger]

Watermelon pillow,
strawberry blanket,

cake in one hand,
lollipop in the other.

Share a story,
share a laugh,
share a little
piece of life.

But love?

Apparently,
that part is private.

Fine.

I'll keep the cake.

[Outro — soft, lightly sarcastic]

Another meeting
without me.

Another romance
right in front of me.

Another secret
I already guessed.

Don't underestimate
these wings.

I see everything.

Well—

almost everything.
